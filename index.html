Research

My thesis and earlier work focus on stochastic modeling and its applications.

Deep learning for solving and estimating the parameters of stochastic differential equations driven by α-stable processes

α-stable processes describe phenomena with jumps and heavy tails, which Brownian motion cannot capture. My thesis aims to solve these equations and estimate their parameters with deep learning, and studies the existence of periodic solutions and their extensions.
